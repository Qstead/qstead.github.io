Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information QStead Group (“we”, “me”) collects through this website and how it is used.

Information you provide

If you use the contact form or email me, I receive the details you submit, such as your name, email address, company, and message. I use this only to respond to your inquiry and to manage the resulting business relationship. Please do not send confidential or proprietary information through the form.

Form processing

Contact form submissions are processed by a third-party form service (Formspree) and delivered to my email. That provider handles the data under its own privacy policy.

Website hosting and fonts

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP address and browser type. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may also receive your IP address when the page loads. I do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

How information is shared

I do not sell your personal information. I share it only with service providers needed to operate the site and respond to you, or when required by law.

Retention and your choices

I keep inquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and maintain business records. You may ask me to access, correct, or delete the personal information I hold about you by emailing qsteadgroup@yahoo.com.

Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision.
