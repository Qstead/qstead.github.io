Global Regulatory Agencies & Standards

A regulatory and standards reference for medical device, pharma, and biotech teams. Click through to any agency's official site.

International Standards & Harmonization

The bodies that set globally referenced standards and coordinate regulatory approaches across countries.

🌐 ISO

International Organization for Standardization

Global

Publishes ISO 13485 (device QMS), ISO 14971 (risk management), ISO 10993 (biocompatibility), and hundreds of other referenced standards.

Visit official site ↗
🌐 ICH

International Council for Harmonisation

Global

Harmonizes pharmaceutical technical requirements through its Q (quality), S (safety), E (efficacy), and M (multidisciplinary) guidelines.

Visit official site ↗
🌐 IMDRF

International Medical Device Regulators Forum

Global

Coordinates device regulatory approaches among major regulators worldwide; successor to the former GHTF.

Visit official site ↗
🌐 WHO

World Health Organization

Global

Sets global health policy and runs the Prequalification Programme many countries rely on for drug and vaccine approval.

Visit official site ↗
🌐 PIC/S

Pharmaceutical Inspection Co-operation Scheme

Global

Harmonizes GMP inspection standards and mutual recognition among roughly 50 member authorities.

Visit official site ↗
🌐 AHWP

Asian Harmonization Working Party

Asia-Pacific

Asia-Pacific counterpart to IMDRF, focused on device regulatory convergence among emerging-market regulators.

Visit official site ↗

Pharmacopeias

Official compendia of drug quality standards and monographs referenced in submissions worldwide.

📖 USP

United States Pharmacopeia

United States

Sets quality standards and monographs for medicines, ingredients, and dietary supplements used in the U.S. and widely referenced abroad.

Visit official site ↗
📖 Ph. Eur.

European Pharmacopoeia (EDQM)

Europe

The pharmacopeia used across EU member states and beyond, maintained by the Council of Europe's EDQM.

Visit official site ↗
📖 JP

Japanese Pharmacopoeia

Japan

Japan's official drug quality compendium, published under the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare.

Visit official site ↗
📖 BP

British Pharmacopoeia

United Kingdom

The UK's official collection of drug quality standards, published on behalf of the MHRA.

Visit official site ↗

Americas

🇺🇸 FDA

Food and Drug Administration

United States

Regulates drugs, biologics, and medical devices in the U.S.; CDER covers drugs and CDRH covers devices.

Visit official site ↗
🇨🇦 Health Canada

Health Canada

Canada

Canada's federal regulator for drugs, medical devices, and health products.

Visit official site ↗
🇲🇽 COFEPRIS

Comisión Federal para la Protección contra Riesgos Sanitarios

Mexico

Mexico's federal sanitary risk protection agency, covering drugs, devices, and food safety.

Visit official site ↗
🇧🇷 ANVISA

Agência Nacional de Vigilância Sanitária

Brazil

Brazil's national health surveillance agency for drugs, devices, and sanitary products.

Visit official site ↗
🇦🇷 ANMAT

Administración Nacional de Medicamentos, Alimentos y Tecnología Médica

Argentina

Argentina's national regulator for medicines, food, and medical technology.

Visit official site ↗
🇨🇴 INVIMA

Instituto Nacional de Vigilancia de Medicamentos y Alimentos

Colombia

Colombia's national institute for drug and food surveillance.

Visit official site ↗
🇨🇱 ISP

Instituto de Salud Pública de Chile

Chile

Chile's public health institute, responsible for pharmaceutical and device regulation.

Visit official site ↗

Europe

🇪🇺 EMA

European Medicines Agency

European Union

Centrally authorizes many medicines across EU member states; devices fall under the EU MDR/IVDR framework with national competent authorities.

Visit official site ↗
🇬🇧 MHRA

Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency

United Kingdom

The UK's independent regulator for medicines and devices, operating separately from the EU since Brexit.

Visit official site ↗
🇨🇭 Swissmedic

Switzerland

Switzerland's regulator for therapeutic products; Switzerland is not an EU member, so it runs its own approval process.
